Google Docs? Word Online? Use the paste page

Some pages can't be edited by any extension — so Nekudot ships its own.

1
Right-click the Nekudot icon and choose "Open paste page".
2
Paste your Hebrew text and click Add Nikud.
3
Copy the dotted text back into your document.
On regular websites you don't need this — Nekudot edits text right in the page, including inside text boxes like Gmail compose.
